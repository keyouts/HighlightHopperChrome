Highlight Hopper — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-15

Summary: Highlight Hopper stores highlights, optional notes, and related metadata locally on your device only. Nothing is transmitted to external servers by the extension.

What we store

  • Highlighted text and the color you chose for it.
  • Optional notes you attach to individual highlights.
  • Metadata to reapply highlights (timestamp, source page, and grouping key).
  • The canonical URL used to group highlights (for example, the page URL or a hyperlink target URL).

Where data is stored

All data is stored in your browser's local extension storage (browser.storage.local). This data remains on your device and is accessible only to the extension.

Sharing and transmission

The extension does not send your highlights, notes, or any personal content to remote servers, third parties, or analytics services.

How to delete your data

You can delete highlights for the current page using the extension's "Clear Highlights" button. To remove all stored highlights and notes, open your browser's extension settings and clear the extension's storage or uninstall the extension.

Permissions

The extension requests the following permissions:

  • storage — to save highlights and notes locally on your device.
  • activeTab — to interact with the page where you create highlights.
  • downloads — to export highlights and notes to a .txt or .csv file when you choose to export.
